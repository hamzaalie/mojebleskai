A Restaurant Rebuild Told as a Transformation Story

Instead of another generic before-and-after card, this case study walks through the actual journey: what was broken, what we built, and what changed in the business.

Balkan Grill House
Location & Type
Prague, Czech Republic — Mid-size dine-in & delivery restaurant, 80 seats, operating 6 years
Services Delivered
Branding Website Mobile App Custom SaaS AI Tools Own Ordering System Loyalty Platform SEO Social Media Photography
Duration & Outcome
10-week transformation — from fragmented tools and third-party dependency to a fully owned, direct-revenue machine.
The Problem

The restaurant looked busy — but the system was quietly bleeding money every single day

  • Bolt Food and Wolt were taking 28–32% commission on every delivery order — costing the owner over €3,200/month in pure platform fees.
  • The website was a 4-year-old template with no online booking, no ordering, and not a single call-to-action that worked. It ranked on page 4 of Google.
  • The brand had no identity — the logo, menu, social media, and signage all looked like they came from different businesses. No consistency, no memorability.
  • There was zero customer retention system. No loyalty program, no email list, no way to bring a guest back after their first visit.
  • Staff scheduling was done on paper. Inventory was tracked in Excel. The owner was spending 3+ hours a day on operational admin instead of running the business.
  • Google reviews were being ignored. 14 unanswered 3-star reviews sat live — silently killing new customer trust.
What We Built

A complete digital and operational rebuild — every service chosen to solve a specific problem

Full Brand Identity

New logo, color system, typography, photography direction, and brand voice. Every touchpoint now looks and feels like one brand.

Custom Website

Mobile-first, SEO-optimized site with direct table booking, digital menus, and a contact funnel. Ranked on Google Page 1 in 6 weeks.

Restaurant Mobile App

iOS & Android app with direct ordering, table reservations, loyalty points, and push notifications. Zero commission. 100% their data.

Own Ordering System

Custom white-label ordering platform replacing Bolt Food and Wolt for direct orders. Saved €3,200/month from day one of launch.

Loyalty & CRM Platform

AI-powered loyalty system that tracks visits, sends automated re-engagement messages, and predicts which customers are about to churn.

AI Chatbot & Booking Agent

24/7 AI assistant on the website and app — handles reservations, answers FAQs, recommends dishes, and collects guest data automatically.

Restaurant SaaS Dashboard

One custom management panel for sales, inventory, staff scheduling, loyalty analytics, and reviews — replacing 7 disconnected tools.

AI Review Management

AI auto-responds to Google & TripAdvisor reviews with brand-consistent replies. All 14 old negative reviews addressed within 48 hours of launch.

AI Inventory & Waste AI

Demand forecasting AI that reduced food waste by 31% in the first month by predicting daily cover counts and adjusting prep quantities.

Local SEO & Google Ads

Full Google Business optimization, local keyword targeting, and paid ad campaigns. Organic impressions up 380% in 8 weeks.

Social Media System

Monthly content calendar, branded templates, reels scripts, and UGC strategy. Instagram follower growth of 2,100 in first 60 days.

Pro Photography & Menu Design
